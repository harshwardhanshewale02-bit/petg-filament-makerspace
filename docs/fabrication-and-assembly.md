# Fabrication & Assembly Log - Step-by-Step Workshop Record

## 1. Fabrication Stages

### Stage 1: Frame & Chassis Assembly
- 2020 aluminum extrusions were cut to specified lengths (350 mm base, 200 mm verticals) using a circular miter saw with a non-ferrous blade.
- T-nuts and corner bracket gussets were tightened with M5 hex screws to ensure torsional rigidity during motor operation.

### Stage 2: Ribbon Slitter Machining
- An internal guide tube was turned on the center lathe to match bottle neck inner diameters.
- High-carbon utility razor blade was mounted with an adjustable screw slot, permitting calibrated lateral ribbon width settings from 7.0 mm to 11.0 mm.
- Dual 608ZZ bearings were press-fitted onto the guide shaft.

### Stage 3: Heater Block & Nozzle Preparation
- A 6061-T6 aluminum billet was milled to 20 × 20 × 12 mm.
- M6 internal threads were tapped for nozzle mating.
- A 6.0 mm H7 bore was drilled for the cartridge heater, along with a 3.0 mm blind hole for the thermocouple bead.
- A standard brass nozzle was modified on the lathe: internal entry was countersunk at 45° to provide a smooth folding funnel leading to the 1.75 mm exit hole.

### Stage 4: Puller Drive & Gearing Assembly
- Stepper motor reduction assembly was mounted on slotted brackets to allow tension adjustment.
- Stainless steel knurled pinch rollers were aligned on 8 mm ground steel dowel pins.
- Spring-loaded idler arm was installed to provide steady clamping force regardless of minor filament thickness variations.

### Stage 5: Cooling & Electronics Integration
- 4010 radial blower was fitted with a 3D printed ABS duct aimed 10 mm downstream of the brass nozzle.
- Wiring harnesses were organized with spiral wrap; heater power lines were sleeved with high-temperature fiberglass insulation.
- Microcontroller and MOSFET / relay driver were mounted in an acrylic enclosure.
