## 1. Safety Measures

### Thermal Hazard Protection (>230°C Hot Surfaces)
- The aluminum heater block and brass extrusion nozzle operate at temperatures up to 260°C.
- A silicone thermal insulation sock and perforated sheet metal guard surround the hotend assembly, preventing accidental skin contact during operation.
- High-temperature warning labels are affixed visibly on the chassis.

### Mechanical Blade Guarding
- The bottle slitting razor blade is mounted inside a shrouded enclosure with an internal clearance of only 1.5 mm, rendering finger contact with the cutting edge virtually impossible while inserting bottles.

### Electrical Isolation & Fusing
- The 24V DC power rails are protected by individual fast-blow automotive blade fuses (5A on heater loop, 2A on motor loop).
- A physical emergency-stop (E-Stop) mushroom button immediately cuts mains AC power to the 24V power supply.

### Fume Ventilation & Air Quality
- Unlike ABS, PETG pultrusion below 260°C generates negligible volatile organic compounds (VOCs).
- Operation is strictly restricted to well-ventilated Makerspace rooms equipped with active overhead exhaust fans.

## 2. Routine Maintenance Schedule

| Maintenance Task | Interval | Procedure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nozzle Cleaning** | Every 50 m of drawing | Cold-pull cleaning to remove charred debris |
| **Blade Inspection** | Every 20 bottles | Inspect cutting edge for nicks; flip or replace razor |
| **Puller Roller Knurl Debris** | Every 100 m | Brush brass wire brush across roller knurls to remove dust |
| **Bearing Lubrication** | Every 20 operating hours | Light machine oil applied to 608ZZ guide bearings |
| **Fastener Tightness** | Monthly | Verify all M5 frame screws and motor bracket bolts |
