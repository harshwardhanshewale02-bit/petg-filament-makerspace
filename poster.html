Problem Statement & Motivation

Global Plastic Pollution Crisis

Over 380 million tonnes of single-use plastics are produced annually. Discarded PET drink bottles choke municipal dumps and waterways worldwide.

High Cost of 3D Printing Filament

Commercial virgin PLA and PETG filament cost ₹1,400 to ₹2,500/kg, creating an unsustainable recurring cost for student innovators and academic makerspaces.

Decentralized Upcycling Solution

Our benchtop pultrusion machine transforms waste bottles directly into high-tensile 1.75 mm filament at ₹0.00/kg raw material cost without industrial pelletization.

02

Project Objectives

1. Precision Ribbon Slitter

Engineered a bearing-guided utility blade fixture to slice bottle sidewalls into uniform 9.0 mm ribbons.

2. Thermal Pultrusion Die

Fabricated an aluminum heater block with conical 1.75 mm nozzle operating at 245°C–260°C.

3. Closed-Loop PID Temperature Control

Implemented thermistor feedback maintaining ±1.5°C thermal stability under air quenching.

4. Geared Stepper Traction Puller

High-torque NEMA17 puller drawing filament at continuous 300–450 mm/min without necking.

5. Print Validation

Verified standard 1.75 ± 0.05 mm diameter tolerance for seamless feeding on open-source FDM 3D printers.

03

Material Science: PET vs. PETG

Molecular Chain Orientation

Blow-molded beverage bottles feature biaxially oriented molecular chains granting superior tensile strength (55–65 MPa). Pultrusion preserves molecular alignment.

PET vs. PETG Glycol Modification

PETG incorporates CHDM glycol to inhibit crystallization. Pure bottle PET is semi-crystalline; it forms at 250°C and requires pre-drying (<0.02% moisture) to prevent hydrolytic degradation.

Thermal Resistance ($T_g$)

Bottle PET has a glass transition temperature of 78°C–82°C (higher than PLA at 55°C–60°C), resulting in parts with superior outdoor and thermal durability.

04

Working Principle & Process Flow

Stage 01 · Collection & Preparation

Clean 1.0L–2.0L beverage bottles washed with surfactant. Labels removed and base cut cleanly.

Stage 02 · Continuous Ribbon Slitting

Bottle mounted on arbor; bearing blade strips continuous 9.0 mm ribbon yielding ~20m per 2L bottle.

Stage 03 · Thermal Pultrusion

Lead-in ribbon drawn through 250°C conical brass nozzle; flat ribbon progressively curls into 1.75 mm solid rod.

Stage 04 · Rapid Air Quenching

Focused 40mm fan drops polymer below $T_g$ immediately upon exit, locking circular tolerance.

Stage 05 · Stepper Puller & Spooling

Planetary geared stepper draws filament with steady traction onto a master 3D-printed spool.

Hardware Prototype Assembly

Integrated Benchtop Workstation

CNC-machined rigid wood baseboard integrates power distribution, control motherboard, LCD panel, and extrusion stage into dedicated, safe zones.

Real-Time Telemetry Interface

Front panel houses 12864 Graphical LCD display with rotary encoder showing live hotend temperature, setpoint, traction RPM, and runtime.

07

Circuits & PID Thermal Control

Power Architecture (24V 10A M-STAR SMPS)

Industrial 240W switch-mode power supply enables rapid preheating (<3 mins to 250°C). AC input secured with fused IEC socket and illuminated safety switch.

Microcontroller & Drivers

Arduino/RAMPS 1.4 executing Marlin-derived firmware with low-$R_{ds(on)}$ MOSFET for heater and A4988 microstepping driver with active cooling.

Closed-Loop PID Regulation

Tuned PID algorithm sampled via NTC 100K 3950 thermistor maintains die temperature within ±1.5°C of 250°C under cooling airflow.

Tolerance Analysis

15-meter extrusion sampling yielded mean diameter 1.748 mm with $\sigma = 0.038\text{ mm}$ and circularity error <3.5%, meeting FDM tolerances.

Sustainability & SDGs

UN SDG 12 · Responsible Consumption

Decentralized circular polymer recycling eliminating petroleum refining and shipping footprints.

UN SDG 13 · Climate Action

Recycling 1 kg of waste PET prevents ~2.3 kg of CO2 equivalent emissions vs virgin polymer production.

Practical Material Yield

1 standard 2.0L bottle yields ~18 to 22 meters of 1.75 mm filament (~40g), enough for 2 structural brackets.
