## 2. Material Property Summary: Recycled PETG

- **Polymer Family:** Thermoplastic copolyester (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol-modified)
- **Density:** $1.38\text{ g/cm}^3$
- **Glass Transition ($T_g$):** $76^\circ\text{C} - 82^\circ\text{C}$
- **Melting Peak ($T_m$):** $250^\circ\text{C} - 260^\circ\text{C}$
- **Tensile Modulus:** $2.8 - 3.1\text{ GPa}$
- **Moisture Absorption:** Up to $0.4\%$ by weight (demands complete oven/air drying prior to drawing)
